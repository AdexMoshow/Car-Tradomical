Install AdexMoshow

Install AdexMoshow on your device to get a full-screen, app-like experience with offline support.

Android
  1. Open this site in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ (three-dot) menu at the top-right.
  3. Tap "Add to Home screen".
  4. Tap "Add" — the AdexMoshow app icon will appear on your home screen.
iPhone / iPad (iOS)
  1. Open this site in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom.
  3. Scroll and tap "Add to Home Screen".
  4. Tap "Add" — the AdexMoshow app icon will appear on your home screen.
Once added, launch AdexMoshow from your home screen like any other app. It works offline and opens in full screen.
